How to use the package

Each talk has 12 main slides, including a five-minute discussion, and two optional backups. The scheduled slot is 20 minutes: 15 presenting and 5 discussing. Companions introduce notation, unpack the mathematics step by step, and give complete citations.

The small analytical illustrations have been checked. The proposed comparative benchmark, neural replication, and organizational pilot have not been run. No general superiority of AI islands or new theorem linking predictive closure to agency is claimed.

Only authored materials are hosted. Downloaded papers, private attachments, and the meeting conversation remain outside the public package. Public repository.
