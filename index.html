How to use the presentations

Each deck has 12 main slides and 2 optional backups, with speaker notes. Each is planned for a 20-minute slot: about 15 minutes presenting and 5 minutes discussing. The companions support a slower reading of the mathematics.

The four-state correction example has been checked by exact enumeration. The proposed transducer study, neural replication, and organizational pilot have not been run. These materials present a conceptual research direction, not a completed theory or established organizational recommendation.

Public reading package. Original papers, unpublished attachments, and the meeting conversation are not hosted here. The presentations use an AI-generated conceptual cover illustration, not a measured scientific figure.
